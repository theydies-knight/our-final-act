# Summary

Five former circus performers—[[Barleighann]], [[Cidrian Lythe|Cidrian]], [[Jaq]], [[Vaelis Duskborn|Vaelis]], and [[Wren 'Sixx' Bran|Wren]]—strolled across [[Eltabbar]] on the first day of Tarsakh. In years past, [[Red Wizards]] crushed their traveling troupe because wearing red violated Thayan law. The five companions maintained an underground speakeasy in the Warehouse Ward. As they wandered into the bustling Bazaar Ward for Market First Day, each adventurer recalled fragments of Eltabbar's grim legacy, from greedy landlords and the City Watch to the demon lord Eltab and the wild magic lurking in the catacombs beneath their feet.

Vaelis scanned the crowded bridge, spotting fine silk bolts from Ven Terrytooth, pricey spyglasses from Val Vann, and jars of rare spices. Barleighann bought fresh fruit for two silver pieces and tossed a peach to Jaq before the group entered Lin's Capes and Cloaks. Shopkeeper [[Teeva Lin]] displayed luxury garments, including a four-thousand-gold *Cape of the Mountebank*. Wren scoffed at the cheaper cloaks and exited the boutique, while Jaq feigned boredom with the stock and urged Barleighann to claim a birthday treat.

Eager to slash the price, Barleighann slid a hidden claw into the Cape of the Mountebank and exposed a fresh tear. Jaq accused Teeva Lin of selling moth-eaten junk while Wren cast Minor Illusion from the street to send a phantom moth fluttering through the shop. Rattled by Jaq's blistering sales pitch, the panicked merchant dropped the price to two thousand gold. Jaq covered half the cost as a gift, and Barleighann distracted Teeva Lin with counter chocolates to slip her nine hundred gold instead of one thousand.

Jaq repaired the torn cape with a quick spell on the street, but their victory lasted mere seconds. A piercing scream shattered the festive bazaar as terrified commoners scrambled across the bridge. Six skeletons with glowing green eyes erupted into the plaza, turning the packed thoroughfare into a maze of fleeing citizens. Barleighann sprang into action with sharp reflexes, seizing a prime spot in the coming brawl as the session closed on a cliffhanger.

## Scenes

### Memories of the Circus & Eltabbar

*Five former circus performers pool their memories of Eltabbar's brutal hierarchy and demonic past.*

- The party established their shared origin in a traveling circus that Red Wizards shattered after the troupe entered Thay wearing red.
- Barleighann (History 8) recalled that Thay's strongest Red Wizards keep homes in Eltabbar, a major hub for magical production and trade.
- Cydrian (History 11) noted the stark gap between wealthy mages and struggling laborers squeezed by greedy landlords.
- Wren (History 15) warned that the City Watch patrols entrances, communicates with the Warehouse Ward fortress, and fines minor offenders, leading locals to handle disputes without the Watch.
- Jaq recounted how Eltabbar's founder allied with the demon lord Eltab in the Abyss to win independence from Mulhorand before Red Wizards betrayed Eltab and shaped the city into a prison.
- Vaelis added that wild magic and Eltab's lingering energy draw aberrations and demons into Eltabbar's catacombs, flagging the Crystal Ball Inn as the top spot for drinks and rest.

### Market Day & the Cloak Shop Con

*The companions browse the Bazaar Bridge stalls before running a brazen scam inside Lin's Capes and Cloaks.*

- Vaelis rolled a 23 on Investigation/Perception to scan the bridge, spotting Ven Terrytooth's silk tent (10–30 GP per bolt), Val Vann's spyglasses (1,000–5,000 GP), and a spice stall.
- Barleighann bought fresh fruit for 2 SP and handed a peach to Jaq before the group entered Lin's Capes and Cloaks.
- Teeva Lin showcased the Cape of the Mountebank (4,000 GP), Cloak of Arachnida (40,000 GP), Cloak of Billowing (100 GP), and Cloak of Many Fashions (100 GP); Wren swished his own Cloak of Many Fashions and walked into the street.
- Jaq lit a cigarette, exited the shop, and pushed Barleighann to treat herself.
- Barleighann used a claw to puncture the Cape of the Mountebank, scoring a dirty 20 on Sleight of Hand to hide the sabotage.
- Jaq blasted the merchant's "moth-eaten" stock while Wren cast Minor Illusion from the window to send a phantom moth fluttering through the shop.
- Aided by Wren's illusion, Jaq rolled a critical 32 on Deception with Advantage, rattling Teeva Lin into slashing the price to 2,000 GP.
- Jaq covered 1,000 GP as a birthday gift, and Barleighann distracted Teeva Lin with counter chocolates (with Jaq's help) to pass 900 GP instead of 1,000 GP on a 17 Sleight of Hand roll with Advantage.
- Jaq cast Mending on the street to fix the claw tear in Barleighann's new cape.

### Skeletons on the Bridge

*Screams scatter the Bazaar Ward crowd as glowing-eyed skeletons emerge to block the party's afternoon stroll.*

- Panicked screams rang across the bridge as six skeletons with glowing green eyes appeared in the market.
- The GM ruled every tile on the bridge as difficult terrain due to the fleeing crowd.
- The party rolled initiative; Barleighann scored a Natural 20, earning the right to pick her turn placement.
- The session paused on the cusp of round one due to player schedule limits.

## NPCs

### Teeva Lin

A cultured human noble who owns Lin's Capes and Cloaks in the Bazaar Ward. Barleighann and Jaq conned her with a fake claw tear and an illusionary moth, dropping the price of a Cape of the Mountebank from 4,000 GP to 1,900 GP.

### Ven Terrytooth

A genial orc textile merchant operating from the northern Bazaar Bridge tents. Vaelis spotted him selling bolts of fine silk in every color except red for 10 to 30 GP.

### Val Vann

A serious, bespectacled human merchant tending the central tents on the Bazaar Bridge. Vaelis noticed her selling high-end spyglasses crafted by her grandfather for 1,000 to 5,000 GP.

### Eltab

An ancient demon lord who helped Eltabbar's founder overthrow Mulhorand before the Red Wizards betrayed him and turned the city into his prison. Though he escaped, his lingering energy draws demons and aberrations into the catacombs.

## Locations

### Eltabbar

Thay's capital city, built on a plateau with Venetian-style canals and stark class divides. Wild magic from Eltab's imprisonment infests its catacombs, while Red Wizards enforce strict laws from towers above.

### Bazaar Ward

A central district of Eltabbar hosting the Market First Day festival on the Bazaar Bridge. Cranes flew over blooming trees until six green-eyed skeletons triggered a stampede.

### Lin's Capes and Cloaks

A fashionable shop along the southern edge of the Bazaar Bridge. Barleighann and Jaq bamboozled its owner out of 2,100 GP in value on a Cape of the Mountebank.

### Warehouse Ward

A working-class district along the canals that houses a military fortress and serves as the party's chosen site for a future speakeasy bastion.

### Crystal Ball Inn

A popular Eltabbar tavern recalled by Vaelis as the prime spot in the city for drinks and lodging.

## Items

### Cape of the Mountebank

A non-attunement magic cape that lets the wearer cast *Dimension Door* a single time per day, leaving a cloud of smoke behind. Priced at 4,000 GP, Jaq and Barleighann acquired it for 1,900 GP through fraud and distraction.

### Cloak of Many Fashions

A 100 GP magic cloak sold at Lin's Capes and Cloaks. Wren wears one of his own and swished it upon exiting the shop.

### Cloak of Arachnida

A rare magic garment priced at 40,000 GP on display inside Teeva Lin's boutique.

### Cloak of Billowing

A common magic garment priced at 100 GP on display inside Teeva Lin's boutique.

### Bazaar Goods

Barleighann bought fresh fruit for 2 SP and gave a peach to Jaq. Vaelis cataloged Ven Terrytooth's silk bolts (10–30 GP), Val Vann's spyglasses (1,000–5,000 GP), and jars of rare spices.

## Spells

### Minor Illusion

Wren cast this cantrip from the street to conjure a fluttering moth inside Teeva Lin's shop, granting Jaq Advantage on her Deception check.

### Mending

Jaq cast this cantrip on the street to repair the claw hole Barleighann tore in the Cape of the Mountebank.
